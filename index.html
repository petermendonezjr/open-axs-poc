OPEN-AXS

Community Energy Resilience, Simplified

An open-architecture energy platform that keeps the lights on when it matters most — powering community resilience hubs with solar, battery storage, and smart load management.

What is a Resilience Hub?

A trusted community facility equipped to provide critical services before, during, and after disruptions to the power grid.

Solar Power

Clean, renewable energy generated on-site from rooftop solar panels — reducing costs and carbon emissions every day.

Battery Storage

Reliable lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries provide hours of backup power — keeping critical loads running when the grid fails.

Smart Management

Automated energy optimization prioritizes critical loads, manages battery cycling, and orchestrates all resources in real time.

How It Works

From sunlight to safety — a seamless energy chain that operates around the clock.

1

Generate

Rooftop solar panels harvest clean energy throughout the day, feeding power directly to the hub.

2

Store

Excess energy charges the battery system, building a resilience reserve for when it's needed most.

3

Optimize

The Open-AXS platform balances solar, battery, and grid resources — minimizing cost and maximizing uptime.

4

Protect

During a grid outage, the hub seamlessly islands — keeping critical loads powered for the community.

E-Mobility & Community Power

Open-AXS integrates bidirectional EV charging and mobile power systems as core infrastructure — not afterthoughts. Electric vehicles become rolling batteries, and mobile power units extend resilience beyond the hub.

  • Bidirectional Level 2 EV charging (V2B capable)
  • Vehicle-to-Building discharge during outages
  • Mobile battery trailers for field deployment
  • Electric bus integration for community transit power
